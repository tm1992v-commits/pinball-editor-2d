# Governance

- **Lead maintainer and project founder:** Carmelo Vadalà. Final approval of changes to the official version.
- **Maintainers:** may be appointed by the lead maintainer; they review and approve pull requests.
- **Official version:** only what is merged into `main` of this repository and released with a version tag.
- **Decisions:** discussed in issues; technical decisions follow tests and reviews; licence decisions are taken by
  the lead maintainer after legal verification.
- **Forks:** allowed by the licence. A fork is not the official version and must not present itself as such.
